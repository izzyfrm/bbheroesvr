Basketball, but you're the hero.

Real hoops. Real hands. Zero limits. You're not watching from the sideline. You're on the hardwood, ball in hand, with the whole arena around you.

It's built VR-first for Meta Quest, so every dribble, every shot and every bit of showboating is something you do with your own body, not a button press. We're building it in the open, so come hang out in the Discord and help shape it.

What it plays like

  • Your hands. Your shot.

    Grab the ball, feel the release, sink it. Basketball that rewards real motion instead of button taps.

  • Superhero energy

    This isn't a sim. It's basketball with a cape: bigger moments, louder highlights and the kind of plays you'll want to clip.

  • Quest native

    Designed from day one for Meta Quest, so it plays like VR should: immersive, comfortable and responsive.

  • Unity 6.6 under the hood

    A modern engine stack, so polish and performance go where VR players feel it most.

  • Built with the squad

    An indie project shaped by its community. Updates, sneak peeks and feedback all happen on Discord and X.
