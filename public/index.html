The game

The ball does what your hands do.

Basketball Heroes is a streetball game built for Meta Quest. You walk onto a city court, pick up a ball and play it the way you would for real.

Nothing is a button prompt. Dribbling, passing and shooting come from how you move your hands, and the ball, the rim and the glass are all physics. Every bounce, rim-out and swish is the result of what you did.

Gameplay

How it plays

Everything runs on the Touch controllers, with your arms doing the work.

  • Grip

    Hold

    Squeeze the grip to take the ball. Point at a loose ball and squeeze to pull it into your hand, or bring your other hand in for a two-hand hold.

  • Push

    Dribble

    Push the ball down with your palm and it comes back up to meet you. Sweep sideways for a crossover, and keep it going while you walk or sprint.

  • Release

    Shoot

    Let go of the grip as you push the ball toward the rim. A ring around the ball shows your power, every release gets a grade, and a scoreboard over the hoop tracks points, field goals and your streak.

  • Jump

    Dunk

    Take it up and slam it through. The rim flexes, your controller buzzes, and you can hang from the rim afterward.

  • Stick

    Move

    Walk, sprint and jump around the court. Turn in 45 or 30 degree steps, or turn smoothly. A comfort jump mode is there if you want it.

  • Menu

    Set it up

    Choose your shot assist (Off, Standard or Casual), shot power, time of day and player skin from the pause menu.
