About the game

Basketball, but you're the hero. Real hoops. Real hands. Zero limits.

Basketball Heroes is a VR basketball game where the court meets the comic book. You're not watching from the sideline — you're standing on the hardwood, ball in hand, with the whole arena around you.

It's built VR-first for Meta Quest, so every dribble, every shot and every moment of showboating is something you do with your own body, not a button press.

Under the hood it runs on Unity 6.6, tuned for the smooth, responsive feel that VR demands. We're building it in the open — come hang out in the Discord and help shape it.
